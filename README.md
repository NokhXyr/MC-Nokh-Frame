<p><span style="font-size:18px;color:#e5e5e5"><strong>Nokh Frame</strong> is a photo studio inside Minecraft. Pose your character with its real cosmetics, or any item, on the backdrop of your choice, and save a clean PNG.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:20px;color:#4cc4cf"><em>Type <code>/nokhframe</code>, pose, click: the photo is ready.</em></span></p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/title-features-en.gif" alt="What It Does" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/prints.gif" alt="Photos of the same player taken in the studio: on a color, on a PNG image, in a 3D scene, in a Blockbench model, then an item, then World mode with a shaderpack" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/shirt.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Your real character:</strong></span> armor, cosmetic layers and your native name tag, as the game draws them.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/sword.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Any item:</strong></span> from Minecraft or any mod, searched by name, ID or <code>@mod</code>.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/smile.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Skin library:</strong></span> try any 64 × 64 PNG; other players still see your own skin.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/brush.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Backdrops:</strong></span> a preset color, any <code>#RRGGBB</code>, or your own PNG image.</p>
</div>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/title-stage-en.gif" alt="Set The Scene" width="800"></h3>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/poses.gif" alt="The pose editor: each click on the Pose button gives the player the next ready-made pose, from neutral to frozen run" width="800"></p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/users.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Poses:</strong></span> nine ready-made poses, or turn each limb on three axes.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/cube.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>3D sets:</strong></span> <code>.nbt</code> structures and Blockbench models built around the player.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/pin.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Placement:</strong></span> move, turn and resize the player, the set or the view.</p>
<p><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/icons/sun.png" alt="" width="32"> <span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:18px"><strong>Shaders:</strong></span> World mode orbits the real camera, so Iris and Oculus shaderpacks apply.</p>

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<h3><img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/NokhXyr/MC-Nokh-Frame@main/images/title-start-en.gif" alt="Getting Started" width="800"></h3>
<p><span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:20px"><strong>1.</strong></span> Put the jar in <code>mods</code>: Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.1 or newer.</p>
<p><span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:20px"><strong>2.</strong></span> Join a world and type <code>/nokhframe</code> in chat.</p>
<p><span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:20px"><strong>3.</strong></span> Pick Player or Item, a backdrop, a set and a pose.</p>
<p><span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:20px"><strong>4.</strong></span> Click <strong>Take photo</strong>: a clean PNG, without the panel, lands in <code>screenshots</code>.</p>
<p><span style="color:#4cc4cf;font-size:20px"><strong>5.</strong></span> On a server with the mod, the studio needs operator rights or the <code>nokhframe.use</code> permission.</p>
</div>
